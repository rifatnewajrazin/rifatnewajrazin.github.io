The B-Sides

The artist manager

When a release is due I'm three inboxes deep at 2am — deadlines, splits, artwork approvals — so the artist only has to think about the music.

The tour manager

Vans, visas, load-ins, day sheets. If the show starts on time and everyone's fed and accounted for, I did the job right.

On two wheels & two feet

Long rides and longer runs are where the actual thinking happens. Half the ideas on this site were worked out somewhere around km 20.

In the friend circle

The designated planner, driver and photo-taker. Someone has to book the table, keep the plan alive, and remember where we left the car.

Introvert / extrovert

Loud in a room of six, invisible in a room of sixty. Both are me — just on different days, and I've stopped apologising for either.
