About

Designing with

I'm a graphic designer and creative professional based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My journey in the creative industry began in 2019, and over the years I've built a versatile career spanning freelance work, part-time roles, and full-time positions across corporate offices and business companies. With 7+ years of professional experience and over 800 clients served, I've had the privilege of collaborating with renowned organizations including Adcomm Ltd. and British American Tobacco (BAT), delivering impactful brand identities and creative design solutions. I currently serve as a Graphic Design Executive at two companies simultaneously — bringing a sharp eye for visual communication and a deep understanding of brand identity to every project.
